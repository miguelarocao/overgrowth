# Easter eggs (spoilers!)

Don't read this if you'd rather find them yourself. 🌱

| Where | What to do | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Any game | Konami code: ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A | 🪩 Disco garden for 15 seconds. The moves the code would have queued are undone for you. |
| Any game | Type `joy` | ✨ Petal confetti in every team's colours. |
| Any game | Type `buzz` (again to dismiss) | 🐝 Bees orbit your mother plant. They are very supportive. |
| Any game | Type `photo` | 📸 The HUD hides for 6 seconds so you can take a screenshot. |
| Rooftops | Find the cat and click it | Purring, hearts. Pet it 5 times for its approval, 20 times and it adopts you. |
| Streets | Click a pigeon | It coos and flies off. Pigeons also scatter when growth reaches them. |
| Plaza fountain | Click it (unless you own it) | 🪙 Toss a coin and get a fortune. |
| Hydrants | Click a water tile you don't own | Splash. |
| Your mother plant | Click it 7 times quickly | 🤧 It sneezes pollen. |
| Shop billboards | Click one | Flip through the city's ads ("WATER YOUR COWORKERS", "PHOTOSYNTHESIZE RESPONSIBLY"…). A few games start with a rare one. |
| Oct 15 – Nov 5 | Look at the rooftops | 🎃 Flickering jack-o'-lanterns. Click one. |
| December | Look at the rooftops | ✨ String lights. |
| Home screen | Click the OVERGROWTH title, a lot | It sprouts. |
| Devtools | Open the console | A greeting, and a gentle note about the map. |
| A wrong URL | Visit any unknown path | The path is overgrown. |
| You own half the city | Just keep growing | The concrete gets nervous. |
